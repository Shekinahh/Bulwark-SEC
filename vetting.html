Initial Training Program

40-Hour Pre-Deployment Curriculum

Before a Bulwark officer steps on any client post, they complete a structured 40-hour in-house training program covering physical security fundamentals, legal powers, report writing, and situational response.

Legal Powers of Arrest
8 hours

Understanding citizen's arrest laws, use of force doctrine, trespass authority, and legal liability for security officers in all operating jurisdictions.

Conflict De-escalation
8 hours

Verbal judo, active listening, behavioral threat recognition, and tactical communication techniques for defusing hostile situations without force.

CPR & Emergency Response
8 hours

American Heart Association (AHA) CPR/AED certification with hands-on manikin practice, bleeding control (TECC), and mass-casualty first-response triage protocols.

Report Writing & Documentation

CCTV & Access Control Systems

Post & Site Orientation
4 hours

Site-specific tour with outgoing shift supervisor, memorizing post orders, emergency evacuation routes, key holder contacts, and radio communication protocols.
